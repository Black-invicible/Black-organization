Academy Curriculum

The Black Academy Method

The Academy is structured around a balanced curriculum in which theoretical knowledge is connected to practical creation and useful application.

Foundation Studies Core academic knowledge, communication, reasoning, and research.
Practical Creation Projects that turn classroom concepts into tangible work.

Creative Development Artistic expression, storytelling, music, design, and innovation.
Enterprise Business thinking, project development, entrepreneurship, and value creation.
Leadership Teamwork, responsibility, communication, discipline, and strategic thinking.
