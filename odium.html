ODIUM

Odium functions as a distribution and sales company for the newly developed chemicals produced by Ovaseas. It ensures that these products reach global markets while educating the public about their applications and benefits.

About Odium

Moving chemical innovation to the world.

Odium connects Ovaseas's chemical production capabilities with customers and markets. Its role is centered on distribution, sales, market reach, and public education about chemical products and their applications.

D

Distribution

Distributes newly developed chemical products from Ovaseas to markets and customers across different regions.

S

Sales

Handles the commercial sales of Ovaseas chemical products and supports their movement from production into the marketplace.

E

Education

Educates the public about chemical products, including their applications and benefits.
