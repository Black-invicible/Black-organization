Storyteller's desk

Good stories begin with a character who wants something and a reason that getting it will not be easy.

  • Give your opening a clear hook.
  • Use paragraphs to control rhythm.
  • Let characters have different voices.
  • Show important moments through action and dialogue.
  • Give the ending a satisfying change or discovery.
