GOLDEN

Golden is a marine and land expansion company that creates new territories and states by reclaiming land from water. It uses advanced AUCHMECHS (Automated Ultrasonic Construction Mechanics) to refill and develop new states, making previously uninhabitable areas livable.

About Golden

Creating new land. Expanding new possibilities.

Golden focuses on marine and land expansion, transforming selected areas that were previously covered by water or considered uninhabitable into developable territories and livable environments.

Core Technology

AUCHMECHS

Automated

Ultrasonic

Ultrasonic technology forms part of the conceptual engineering system used for Golden's marine and land reclamation operations.

Construction Mechanics

Mechanical construction systems are used to transform reclaimed spaces into structured and potentially livable territories.

Territory Development
