The Vision

Built for the
Unprecedented.

CALCULUS is designed around one idea: major events should have a venue capable of matching their ambition.

A destination for the biggest nights, the biggest stages and the biggest crowds.

Designed as a landmark event venture, CALCULUS brings together live music, large-scale entertainment, cultural gatherings, festivals, ceremonies and major public events under one extraordinary concept.

With a stated capacity of 5 million people, CALCULUS is positioned as one of the largest event ventures in the world — a place where the audience itself becomes part of the spectacle.
