Black Organization is a movement of visionaries, creators, and innovators building a better future through creativity, technology, and purpose.

What started as a handful of ideas has grown into a roster of nearly forty companies — spanning software, fashion, music, laundry services, publishing, and more — each one formally built around a clear mission and standard.
