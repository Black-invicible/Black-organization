Our History

From Headquarters
to Meeting Center

Golden Empire International's role changed in 2026 — its address in the organization didn't.

Founding

Golden Empire International

Founded by CT Black as the overall headquarters — the single address every part of the organization operated under.

Operating Years

The Overall Headquarters

Golden Empire International served as the seat of the organization, housing leadership and coordinating every division under one name.

2026

Restructuring

The organization restructured under a new name — Black Organization — with each division formalized as its own subsidiary.

2026 — Present

Central Meeting Center

Golden Empire International was repurposed as Black Organization's central meeting center — the strategic hub where staff across every subsidiary meet to decide, plan, and discuss.
