Golden Empire World Health Organization

Global Healthcare

Healthcare for everyone.

GE-WHO provides a global healthcare framework intended to make medical treatment accessible across borders while establishing clear provisions for Nigerian citizens, foreign nationals, and Nigerian Civilian Certificate holders.

01

Global Healthcare

GE-WHO is structured to provide healthcare services for people across different countries and territories.

02

Nigerian Citizens

Nigerian citizens receive full medical care through GE-WHO at no cost.

03

Privileged Individuals

Privileged individuals are eligible for free treatment under the organization's healthcare provisions.

Healthcare Access Charter

Care without borders

GE-WHO's healthcare policy distinguishes access according to the person's citizenship and recognized status within the system.

Nigerian Citizens Full medical care at no cost.
Foreign Nationals / Non-Nigerian Civilians Medical treatment is available on a paid basis.
NCC Holders Holders of a Nigerian Civilian Certificate receive free treatment abroad under GE-WHO's stated provisions.
Privileged Individuals Eligible individuals receive free treatment as provided by GE-WHO.
GE-WHO Vision

Health should travel with the person.

GE-WHO is designed as an international healthcare network in which recognized citizens and certificate holders can access medical care beyond their home territory, while maintaining a defined framework for international patients.
