No. 6 — Floret Bank of Naija

Floret Bank of Naija

A financial institution focused on e-currency and hard-currency transactions. It serves as the exclusive handler of HONDS, the currency created by CT BLACK, and is responsible for salary payments and financial transactions for the BLACK Organization.

Core Financial Role

Floret Bank of Naija provides the financial infrastructure that supports the BLACK Organization's internal economy and its wider financial operations.

E-Currency

Handles digital and electronic currency operations within the BLACK Organization's financial ecosystem.

Hard Currency

Supports transactions involving physical and internationally recognized currencies where required.

HONDS Management

Acts as the exclusive financial handler of HONDS, the currency created by CT BLACK for the BLACK Organization.
