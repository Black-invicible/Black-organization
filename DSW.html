DSW
Designing Your World, one mark at a time

Designwurld is a graphics and design studio built on a simple belief — good design should feel deliberate, layered, and unmistakably yours.

As the design arm of Black Organization, DSW works across the group's own subsidiaries and for outside clients, taking on branding, print, packaging, and digital design with the same studio discipline in every project.

From a single logo mark to a full identity system, the process stays hands-on — concepts are sketched, stacked, and refined until the design earns its place on the page.
