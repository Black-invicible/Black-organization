PROGRAMS

Support systems designed to strengthen Black communities at every stage.

YOUTH

Leadership Academy

An intensive leadership development experience that teaches advocacy, communication, and strategy to young people preparing to lead in school, work, and community spaces.

BUSINESS

Black Enterprise Lab

A business support network that helps founders refine ideas, build operational tools, strengthen customer strategies, and access the right resources for sustainable growth.

CARE

Community Care Network

A neighborhood-based support system connecting families and individuals with essential care, resource navigation, and community-led support services.

CIVIC

Advocacy & Policy

We equip organizers and residents with tools to engage in civic education, issue advocacy, and public policy conversations that shape community life.
