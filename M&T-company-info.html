MACROPOLO & TAUNDER (M&T)

Heavy Machinery Manufacturing for Industrial, Agricultural, and Construction Use

About M&T

M&T is a heavy machinery manufacturing company specializing in the production of industrial, agricultural, and construction machines. It develops advanced mechanical and automated heavy equipment for global use, with a focus on innovation and durability.

List of Heavy Machines Produced by M&T

  • Excavators
  • Bulldozers
  • Graders
  • Gantry Cranes
  • Loaders: Backhoe Loaders, Skid Steer Loaders, Wheel Loaders, etc.
  • Tractors: Agricultural, Industrial, and Compact Tractors
  • Dump Trucks & Tippers
  • Compactors & Rollers
  • Asphalt Pavers
  • Forklifts
  • Graders & Scrapers
  • Drilling Machines: Rock Drills, Piling Rigs, etc.
  • Concrete Batching Machines: Batching Plants
  • Concrete Pumps & Mixers
  • Mining Equipment: Draglines, Hydraulic Mining Shovels, etc.
  • Logging Machines: Feller Bunchers, Harvesters, etc.

Our Commitment

M&T focuses on engineering machines that enhance productivity across various industries, combining strength, precision, and smart automation for global infrastructure growth.
